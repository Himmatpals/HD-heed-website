Master scripts for Support Coordinators and LACs

First email — Support Coordinators / LACs
Subject: Local NDIS support option for Penrith and Hawkesbury Hi [First name / Team], I’m Himmat from HD HeeD. We support NDIS participants across Richmond/Hawkesbury, Penrith and surrounding Western Sydney areas. I’m reaching out because your role is often where local service choices and support navigation happen quickly. We aim to make referrals easier by being clear about: - who we can support - where we operate - how quickly we respond - the best intake contact for urgent or routine enquiries If useful, I can send you a one-page referral guide for your team. If there is a current service gap you are finding hard to place locally, I would also be happy to indicate whether HD HeeD may be a suitable fit. Kind regards, Himmat HD HeeD
First call opener — Support Coordinator
Hi, this is Himmat from HD HeeD. We support NDIS participants around Richmond/Hawkesbury and Penrith. I’m reaching out because support coordinators often know very quickly where local service gaps are. I am not calling for an exclusive arrangement. I just want to make sure your team knows who we are and when HD HeeD may be a suitable local option. Would it be okay if I ask one quick question? What type of local support is hardest for you to place right now? [Listen and write the answer.] That is helpful. If I send a one-page referral guide with our service area, response process and intake contact, would that be useful? Close: Thank you. I will send it through and only follow up if helpful.
First call opener — LAC / NDIS front door
Hi, this is Himmat from HD HeeD. We are a local NDIS provider supporting participants around Richmond/Hawkesbury and Penrith. I understand LACs support participant choice and connection, so I am not asking for direct referrals or anything exclusive. I’m simply trying to find the correct person or pathway for sharing a short local provider information sheet. Who would be the best person to receive local provider information for participants and families who ask about support options in the area? If they allow: Thank you. I can send a concise one-page guide with our service area, intake contact and support types. What email should I use? If they decline: No problem. I understand. Thank you for your time.
Follow-up email after call
Subject: HD HeeD local provider guide Hi [Name], Thank you for speaking with me today. As discussed, HD HeeD supports NDIS participants across Richmond/Hawkesbury, Penrith and surrounding Western Sydney areas. We may be suitable where a participant or family needs local support such as: - daily living support - personal care - community access and social participation - carer relief / respite-style support - clinically informed communication where appropriate A safe first enquiry can be general only: - suburb - support type required - urgency - funding type if known - preferred contact person Please only send participant details where consent and the correct referral pathway are in place. Kind regards, Himmat HD HeeD

Safe outreach rules

Do not do this

  • Do not pressure LACs or coordinators for referrals.
  • Do not offer commissions or exclusive referral arrangements.
  • Do not ask for participant health details during marketing outreach.
  • Do not add people to bulk SMS unless consent is clear.
  • Do not keep contacting anyone who asks to stop.

Do this instead

  • Use information-only language: “local provider guide”.
  • Respect participant choice and control.
  • Ask about service gaps before selling.
  • Keep a do-not-contact field in your tracker.
  • Move actual referrals into a separate participant intake system.
