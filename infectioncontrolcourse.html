2. Required before first participant-facing shift

Send clear scanned copies/photos by email, or provide paper copies to HD HeeD.

First Aid and CPR

Preferred for basic low-risk support. Required before high-risk or complex supports if requested by HD HeeD, participant risk, funding requirements, insurer requirements or the specific service arrangement.

National Police Check

Not required where a current NDIS Worker Screening Clearance is confirmed, unless HD HeeD requests it for a specific service arrangement, insurer requirement, aged care setting or participant risk.

3. Download and sign acknowledgements

Please open each policy page, print/save it if needed, sign the acknowledgement section, and return a scanned copy/photo or paper copy to HD HeeD.

Required NDIS Code of Conduct acknowledgement Expected behaviour, respect, safety, privacy and reporting obligations. Open acknowledgement
Required Privacy and confidentiality acknowledgement No sharing participant information, photos, documents or details without authority. Open acknowledgement
Required Incident reporting process acknowledgement Worker knows what must be reported immediately and who to contact. Open acknowledgement

4. Training and certificates

1
NDIS Worker Orientation Module — Quality, Safety and You

Provide your completion certificate. If you have not completed it, use the official NDIS Commission training link below.

Open NDIS Worker Orientation Module
2
HD HeeD Infection Prevention and Control Induction

Complete the HD HeeD internal induction, pass the questions, then print/save and send the completion certificate to HD HeeD.

Open HD HeeD infection control induction
3
Other certificates if available

Send First Aid, CPR, manual handling, medication assistance, hoist training or other relevant certificates if you already have them. HD HeeD may require extra certificates before high-risk or complex shifts.

5. Additional information for contractors

If you are engaged as a contractor, HD HeeD may also request:

  • ABN details
  • contractor agreement
  • insurance evidence if required
  • invoice/payment details
  • confirmation that you understand you must still follow HD HeeD policies, participant instructions and reporting requirements

6. Tick what you are attaching/sending

7. How to submit

1
Complete this page

Fill your details and tick the documents/certificates you are sending.

2
Download or print the completed intake summary

Use “Download completed summary” or “Print / Save completed page as PDF”.

3
Email documents to HD HeeD

Email the completed summary and all required documents to info@hdheed.com.au. If emailing is not possible, provide paper copies directly to HD HeeD.

Do not attend a shift until confirmed

Submitting documents does not automatically approve you to work. HD HeeD must review and confirm your pre-shift readiness first.
