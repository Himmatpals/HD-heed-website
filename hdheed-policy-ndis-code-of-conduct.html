NDIS Code of Conduct

Minimum worker expectations for HD HeeD staff and support workers delivering NDIS supports.

Version 1.0Effective: 26 May 2026Review: 26 May 2027

Purpose

This policy explains the minimum conduct expected from every HD HeeD worker. It applies to staff, contractors, casual workers, volunteers and anyone delivering supports on behalf of HD HeeD.

Workers must behave in a way that protects participant rights, dignity, safety and wellbeing.

Worker expectations

HD HeeD workers must:

  1. Act with respect for participant rights, choice, control and privacy.
  2. Deliver supports safely, competently and with care and skill.
  3. Act with integrity, honesty and transparency.
  4. Promptly report and respond to any concerns about violence, abuse, neglect, exploitation or discrimination.
  5. Never use unauthorised restrictive practices.
  6. Maintain professional boundaries and avoid conflicts of interest.
  7. Follow HD HeeD procedures, participant care instructions and reasonable directions.
  8. Ask for guidance if unsure, instead of guessing or taking unsafe action.
Simple rule: treat every participant with the same respect, safety and dignity you would expect for your own family member.

Unacceptable conduct

Workers must not:

  • shout at, threaten, mock, ignore or intentionally embarrass a participant;
  • provide care in a rough, unsafe or disrespectful manner;
  • borrow money, accept inappropriate gifts or manage participant money unless authorised by HD HeeD;
  • take photos/videos of a participant without written authority;
  • share participant information outside authorised care communication;
  • attend shifts affected by alcohol, drugs, fatigue or illness;
  • leave a participant unsafe or unattended when support is required.

Immediate reporting

Report immediately to the HD HeeD manager/RN if there is any concern involving harm, risk, abuse, neglect, exploitation, injury, medication issue, unsafe environment, missing participant, complaint or conflict.

Emergency: call 000 first if there is immediate danger, serious injury, urgent medical concern, fire, threat or police/ambulance need. Then notify HD HeeD as soon as safe.
