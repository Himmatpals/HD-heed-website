Important pricing notes

NDIS price limits Service agreement first Travel by agreement Cancellations may apply

Prices shown are public-facing NDIS price limits for common HD HeeD supports. They are not a guarantee that a support can be delivered or funded. Before services start, HD HeeD confirms the participant’s goals, support needs, risk level, funding category, schedule, location, staff availability, travel arrangements and service agreement.

Where a rate, travel cost, cancellation rule, or support item changes under the NDIS, the current NDIA pricing arrangements take priority.
