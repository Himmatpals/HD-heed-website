Important pricing notes

These rates are based on common NDIS 2025-26 price limits for NSW / National MMM 1-5 areas. The final supports, schedule, travel, cancellation rules and payment arrangements must be confirmed directly with HD HeeD before supports start.

NDIS price limits are maximum prices. Where applicable, providers and participants can agree a lower rate. Always check the current NDIS Pricing Arrangements and Price Limits before confirming supports.
