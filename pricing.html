Important pricing notes

NDIS price limitsService agreement firstTravel by agreementCancellations apply

Prices shown are maximum NDIS price limits for NSW / National MMM 1–5 areas and may not apply to remote or very remote locations. Some supports may require evidence, plan funding, participant consent, clinical information, a risk review, or a support coordinator / plan manager discussion before HD HeeD can safely commence services.

HD HeeD may charge below the listed price limit where agreed. Any final quote depends on the participant’s needs, funding category, support ratio, staff skill level, location, requested schedule, and service agreement.
